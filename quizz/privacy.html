Quizz Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Overview

Quizz is a trivia game provided by Rajendra Sharma as a free, ad-supported service and is intended for use as is. This page explains what information the app uses and the choices you have.

What we do not collect

Quizz does not ask you to create an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves some information on your device only: your last nickname, your streaks, XP, achievements and stats, which categories you have unlocked, your reminder setting, and a copy of the question bank. This stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Multiplayer games

When you host or join a live game, the app sends your nickname, the game code and your answers to our game server so the game can run. This is kept in the server's memory only while the game is active and is discarded when the game ends (a game expires after at most one hour).

Our hosting provider and server logs may briefly record technical information such as your IP address, connection times, and the nicknames and game codes used in a game. We do not use this to identify you.

Questions and update checks

The app downloads new questions from our website and checks for the latest app version. As with any internet request, these requests reveal your IP address to those servers.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, Quizz schedules a notification on your device. It is created on the device itself, and no information about it is sent to us.

Advertising

Quizz is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Most ads are optional: you choose to watch a rewarded ad, for example to unlock a category or earn a bonus. A full-screen ad may also appear after a multiplayer game.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Quizz asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time with the Ad choices link at the bottom of the home screen (shown in those regions). Everywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads).

Sharing from the app

When you tap Share, Quizz passes text to your phone's own share menu. You choose where it goes, and we do not receive it.

Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because Quizz has no accounts, there is no account to delete. You can clear the app's data, change your ad consent, reset your advertising ID, or turn off the daily reminder at any time. If you are in the EEA or UK you may have rights under data protection law, such as access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. We hold very little, but you are welcome to ask using the contact below.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data we handle, even though it is limited and mostly anonymous.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

If you have any questions, you can contact us at hello@quantixlabs.org.
