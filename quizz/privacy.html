Quizz Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Overview

Quizz is a trivia game provided by Rajendra Sharma as a free, ad-supported service and is intended for use as is. This policy explains, in general terms, what information the app uses and the choices you have.

Information we do not require

Quizz does not require you to create an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information on your device

The app stores some information on your device, such as your settings, preferences and progress. This stays on your device unless it is needed to provide a feature you choose to use. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Online features

Some features connect to our servers or website, for example to play with others, to receive new content, or to check for updates. These connections may involve information such as a nickname you choose, your activity within a game, your IP address, and your device and app version. This information is used to provide and secure those features and is not kept permanently. Our servers and hosting providers may keep technical logs for security and troubleshooting.

Advertising

Quizz is free and is supported by ads shown through Google AdMob. Ads may appear in different formats and at different points in the app. Some, such as rewarded ads, are ones you choose to watch in exchange for a benefit in the app.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Quizz asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time with the Ad choices option in the app (shown in those regions). Everywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your device settings.

Third-party services

The app relies on third-party services, such as Google AdMob for advertising and hosting providers for our servers and website. These services handle information under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Optional features may ask for permissions on your device, for example to show notifications. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because Quizz has no accounts, there is no account to delete. You can clear the app's data, change your ad consent, reset your advertising ID, or change app permissions at any time. If you are in the EEA or UK you may have rights under data protection law, such as access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. We hold very little, but you are welcome to ask using the contact below.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data we handle, even though it is limited.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

If you have any questions, you can contact us at hello@quantixlabs.org.
